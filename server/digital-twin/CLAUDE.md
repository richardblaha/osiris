# CLAUDE.md — digital twin

Details of `server/digital-twin` (API) and `server/digital-twin/web` (PWA); the repo-wide picture is in the root CLAUDE.md.

```bash
pnpm --filter @osiris/digital-twin dev   # API :3004, PWA :5175; needs a Kubernetes API (KUBE_*) and ClickHouse (CLICKHOUSE_*)
```

**Overview** (`server/digital-twin` + `server/digital-twin/web`, namespace `osiris`, https://twin.k6labs.cz): a general digital twin system as a 3D WebGL PWA (three.js, the OSIRIS theme in cyan and magenta) — nested translucent shells of what each twin contains, gaps between siblings, edges coloured by status. **Models are DTDL v3** (`@osiris/shared/dtdl.ts`: types, `interfaceErrors`, `resolveContents` with `extends`, `twinErrors`/`relationshipErrors`, localized `displayName`s) and **instances follow the Azure Digital Twins API**.

**Twins (instances).** It holds any number of twins (table `instances`, `src/instances.ts` `Instances` runs each one's source, sync, history and telemetry and restarts it when changed): each is a kind of source from the **registry** (`src/sources/registry.ts` `SOURCE_TYPES`: fields of its settings form, secret fields write-only and sealed with `TWIN_SECRETS_KEY` (`SecretBox`), a zod schema, `create` → adapter + `Telemetry`; today `kubernetes`: the cluster the service runs in or a pasted kubeconfig (`parseKubeconfig`: token only, inline only, no files), Ceph managers, its telemetry's ClickHouse (empty: the service's own) and cluster name), managed by admins (`/api/source-types`, `/api/admin/instances[/:id]`, the PWA's `Instances.tsx` behind the twin menu in the bar, `InstanceMenu.tsx`); `/api/instances` lists the twins the user may see, and every twin route below lives under `/api/instances/:instance` (the PWA's `api.ts` prefixes them with the chosen twin, kept in the URL as `twin=` and in `localStorage`). The first start with none makes one from the old one-twin settings (`src/bootstrap.ts`: `CLUSTER_NAME` as its id, so its history goes on; it takes the one twin's dashboards and filters, `claimLegacy`, and the Osiris dashboard; once — deleted, it stays deleted).

**API** in the shape of Azure Digital Twins (`$dtId`, `$etag`, `$metadata.$model`; relationships `$relationshipId`/`$sourceId`/`$relationshipName`/`$targetId`): `/api/models[/:id]`, `/api/digitaltwins/:id` with `/relationships` and `/incomingrelationships`, plus the lists `/api/digitaltwins` and `/api/relationships` (`?at=` a past time), `/api/digitaltwins/:id/history`, `/api/history/range` and the models' telemetry `/api/telemetry/errorLogs`, `/api/telemetry/storage` (each volume claim's filesystem: `used`, `fsCapacity` from kubeletstats' `k8s.volume.capacity`/`available`, named by the claim only with the agent's `extra_metadata_labels: [k8s.volume.type]`, the adapter's `storageMetrics`, `Telemetry.latest`) and `/api/telemetry/network` (bytes/s of nodes and pods, the adapter's `networkMetrics`: kubeletstats' `k8s.node.network.io`/`k8s.pod.network.io`, rated over 5 minutes; `Telemetry.rates`; a twin without its own counter, like a microk8s node, carries its children's in the view).

**Models and sync.** Every model extends `dtmi:osiris:twin:Entity;1` (`name`, `status`, `otelResource` = the OpenTelemetry resource attributes of its telemetry, Telemetry `errorLogs`). A **source adapter** (`TwinSource` in `src/twin.ts`: `models()`, `snapshot()`, `errorAttributes`, `networkMetrics`, `storageMetrics`) reads a system; `src/sync.ts` validates its models and every `SYNC_INTERVAL_SECONDS` writes the snapshot into **SQLite** (`src/store.ts`, `DATA_DIR/twin.db`, schema 2: `TwinStore` holds the instances, `of(id)` an `InstanceData` with that twin's models, twins, relationships, meta, dashboards and saved filters; one replica with its PVC, `strategy: Recreate`) and sends what changed as **OpenTelemetry events** (`src/history.ts` `HistoryLog`, `eventEmitter` of `@osiris/platform/telemetry.ts`: `twin.entity`/`twin.relationship` log records with `twin.id` = the instance, `dt.*`/`rel.*` attributes and `dt.ancestors`, a checkpoint of everything once a day) into ClickHouse's `otel_logs`; `HistoryReader` rebuilds any time from the newest checkpoint plus the changes (30 days, the logs' TTL).

**The 3D view.** The PWA (`web/src/scene.ts`) builds the view only from DTDL: `contains` nests twins, statuses and `errorLogs` roll up, labels and property formatting come from the models; children sit in a cube-like 3D grid (`arrange`/`cubeGrid` in `TwinLayout.ts`: columns, rows and layers from the floor up, groups by model lined up along the axis that keeps the whole nearest a cube; leaves are cubes), so the twin is a block rather than a plate; boxes have slightly rounded edges and storage (a model with Telemetry `used`) is a cylinder filled as far as it is full; twins whose model declares Telemetry `network` get a line from a hub on their container's back wall to their wall facing it with packets moving by their load, related siblings (a container `mounts` a claim) a line wall to wall, and the cluster's hub reaches the internet, a globe behind it, by the sum of its nodes' traffic — every line ends on a wall, mantle or the globe, marked as an interface, never inside a solid (`TwinLayout.ts`, `Twin3D.tsx`); a twin outside the containment tree that relates to one inside it groups it (`project` in `scene.ts`): the outermost group, a namespace, is a band — one dashed solid across the cluster around all of it, in every node and outside them (`measureBanded` in `TwinLayout.ts`: the nodes and Ceph are columns, each band a row across them, what no node holds a wall-less column, what no band holds the last row; the band rows go one behind another in levels stacked as high as keeps the cluster nearest a cube) — and the inner groups, workloads, are projections in each node (their telemetry is their group's on the node); each kind of twin has its own look (`lookOf` in `icons.ts`: the cluster on a floor grid, a node with a status light, a namespace dashed and tinted, a workload dotted, a container solid, storage a filled cylinder, a disk with platters, an interface a port on its container's wall, a Service a hexagonal outline, an Ingress a gate), interfaces are wired (`wire`, `overlay` dashed for VXLAN), routing is drawn only on the selection's traffic path (`pathOf`: public address → LoadBalancer → controller pods, Ingress → Service → pods → eth0 → router, lit with packets), a legend lists the looks and switches layers (network, storage, groupings; `Legend.tsx`); every solid has a label with its official Kubernetes icon as an outline (`k8s-icons.ts`, generated by `web/scripts/k8s-icons.mjs` from kubernetes/community's icon set, Apache-2.0 or CC-BY-4.0; `icons.ts`), its name, kind and state, shown by level of detail (the solid's size on screen); the 3D view is built once and repaints on refresh. Selected and hovered things glow in a bright cyan (`--accent-active`), errors are magenta (the twin's `--state-error`); interfaces with an address (pods, nodes, disks) show it beside their dot by level of detail.

**Interaction.** Hover names a box; a click selects it and the camera flies to center it with room around it, with HTML panels in the corners (`FocusPanels.tsx`: properties, a log tail of the 10 newest that loads older ones as it scrolls up, CPU/memory/network sparklines, traces) joined to the box by SVG lines redrawn every frame; the first Escape closes the panels (the camera stays), the next flies back to the whole twin. A time axis (`Timeline.tsx`) and each twin's Changes.

**Kubernetes adapter.** The one adapter is Kubernetes (`src/sources/kubernetes/`, models `;3`: namespaces, pods, nodes, replicasets → `contains` is physical: cluster → nodes (and unscheduled pods) → pods → containers and the PersistentVolumeClaims they mount (`PersistentVolumeClaim;1`, in the first pod by name, an unmounted one in the cluster; containers `mounts` it; the ClusterRole lists `persistentvolumeclaims`, without it the twin goes without them); the network (`network` in `graph.ts`, model `dtmi:osiris:net:Interface;1` with `kind` physical/router/tunnel/veth/loadbalancer/public): each node's card, Calico router (it routes, no bridge) and VXLAN tunnel (from the node's Calico annotations), each pod's `eth0` wired to its node's router (`connectedTo`), Services (`Service;1`, `routesTo` the pods of their EndpointSlices, a warning without ready ones) with their LoadBalancer addresses (MetalLB, `exposes`), Ingresses and Traefik IngressRoutes (`Ingress;1`, `routesTo` Services, `servedBy` the controller's LoadBalancer, an error for a missing Service), and the public addresses the Ingress hosts resolve to (`publicAddressesOf`; Cloudflare's addresses become one CDN interface in front of the cluster's own); namespaces and workloads relate by `hasNamespace`/`hasWorkload`/`hasPod`/`hasClaim`; with `CEPH_MANAGERS` (the managers' Prometheus pages, the active one answers; `sources/kubernetes/ceph.ts`) Ceph is a twin in the cluster with its pools (`holds` the claims whose PV is an image in it) and its OSDs as disks (cylinders) in the nodes they're in — the manager publishes no per-OSD bytes, so a disk's fill is the cluster's average and carry their worst pod's status; ids are escaped paths `k8s:<cluster>/namespace/<ns>/pod/<pod>`).

**Pages and telemetry.** Telemetry and history answers are cached in the process (`memoryCache`). The twin is the PWA's only view under a see-through bar (pages centered, the user an avatar with a menu); the telemetry pages (Logy, Metriky, Trasy — the same ClickHouse queries the dashboard reads with) lie half see-through over it, closed by their tab again or Escape, and open filtered by the selected twin's `otelResource` (logs, traces and series take `namespace`, `pod`, `node`, `container`, `claim`). The first tab, Overview, opens a dashboard (with several, a menu asks which): named, shared dashboards (`/api/dashboards`, table `dashboards`, `Dashboard.tsx`) of panels — log bar charts, log records, metrics, traces — each with its page's filter and a width of four columns, over one time range; the pages add their current view to one ("+ Overview"). Logs show a bar chart of records over time by severity (`/api/logs/histogram`; hover shows a bar's counts, a click picks its time and level, a drag a span) and the user's saved filters (`/api/me/filters`, table `saved_filters` in `twin.db`, each user's own); their filters (service, namespace, node, pod, container from `/api/facets`, level, text searched as it's typed) live in the URL. A record's severity is its own, else read from its text (klog's prefix, a `level` field, the level's word, a Warning event; `SEVERITY_EXPR` in `telemetry.ts`), else info: most pods' output carries none, and the twin's error counts use the same. Without a token the PWA goes straight to sign-in (once a minute at most, so a failed sign-in never loops). The twin seeds an "Osiris" dashboard once (`src/dashboards.ts`).

**Osiris chat.** Osiris's agent lives over the twin (`OsirisChat.tsx`): the input at the bottom in the middle, the conversation above the twin, voice messages (transcribed by Osiris, Mistral), answers read aloud (Piper), approvals as forms, and `twin_view`'s steps run on the view (select, turn/tilt/zoom through Twin3D's `control`, open pages, history); it calls Osiris through the twin's `/osiris/api/*` proxy (`OSIRIS_URL`, project `OSIRIS_PROJECT`) with the user's token, which osiris-gateway must accept from the `digital-twin` client.

**Sign-in and storage.** Sign-in is osiris-auth's: the PWA signs in as the public client `digital-twin` (PKCE), the API introspects as `osiris-digital-twin`: the group `admin` (`AUTH_ADMIN_GROUP`) sees and manages every twin, the group `twin:<id>` sees that twin (`maySee` in `auth.ts`; another twin answers 404), anyone else gets 403. `CLICKHOUSE_*` is the service's own ClickHouse (the history's, and a twin's telemetry when it names none); a twin's telemetry cluster name is the `k8s.cluster.name` its collector's resource/cluster processor stamps telemetry with. Controlling the twinned system (DTDL `Command`s, desired state, audit) would go to PostgreSQL.
